# HopeSpace Aaral AI 🌱

## 🧠 About the Project

HopeSpace Aaral AI is a healthcare technology solution designed to support people dealing with post-trauma depression and emotional well-being.

Our platform aims to provide a safe digital space where users can connect, express their feelings, and access emotional support through technology.
